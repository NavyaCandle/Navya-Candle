What buyers say

We started with one burner and a bad candle

In 2022 we bought a ₹700 "luxury" candle that tunnelled down the middle and left soot on the wall. Navya came out of trying to fix that in a home kitchen in Delhi — 40-odd test pours before the first one burned flat to the edge.

Today a team of six pours every batch by hand. We use American soy wax, cotton wicks, and IFRA-certified fragrance oils, and we cure each jar ten days before it goes near a box.

Common questions

How long does delivery take?

Dispatch is 2–3 working days; delivery 3–6 days depending on the pin code.

How do I pay?

Add candles to your cart and tap Order on WhatsApp. Your order details open in WhatsApp, we confirm them and share the UPI payment details right there.

Is cash on delivery available?

No. Candles are fragile and COD returns usually arrive broken, so we ship prepaid only.

What if my candle arrives broken?

Record an unedited opening video with no pauses or cuts and send it within 48 hours. We replace or refund, your choice.

Do you deliver outside India?

For international orders, please message us on WhatsApp and we will help you with availability and shipping.

Do you do custom labels?

Yes, from 50 units. Send your logo as PNG or PDF and we'll share a print proof before production.
