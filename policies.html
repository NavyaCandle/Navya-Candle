Shipping, refunds, privacy & terms

Last updated: September 2026

Shipping policy

We ship across India. Orders are dispatched within 2–3 working days of payment being confirmed, and delivery usually takes 3–6 days after dispatch depending on your PIN code. Shipping is a flat ₹165 anywhere in India. You can follow your order on our site with your order number and mobile number.

Outside India. For international orders, please message us on WhatsApp and we will help you with availability and shipping.

Candles are made by hand in small batches, so during festive seasons dispatch can take a little longer. If that happens we will message you.

Cancellation, refund & breakage policy

Payment. All orders are prepaid. We do not offer cash on delivery. After you place an order it opens in WhatsApp, and we share the UPI payment details there. Your order is confirmed once we receive the payment.

Cancelling. You can cancel an order before it is dispatched by contacting us with your order number. We will refund the full amount.

Broken or damaged items. Record one unedited video of opening the parcel, with no pauses or cuts, and send it to us within 48 hours of delivery. We will replace the item or refund you, whichever you prefer.

Refund timing. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method and normally reach you within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

Custom orders. Products made with your logo or label are made specially for you and cannot be cancelled once the print proof is approved.

Privacy policy

What we collect. To deliver your order we collect your name, email, mobile number and delivery address, and the items you bought. If you join our newsletter we keep your email address. If you write a review we keep the name, city and text you submit.

Payments. You pay by UPI to the details we share on WhatsApp. We never ask for your card or bank details, and this site has no server storing your data — your order details are sent directly to us on WhatsApp.

How we use it. Only to process and deliver orders, answer your messages, send the newsletter you signed up for, and meet legal and tax requirements. We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the services needed to fulfil your order, such as the courier and the payment provider.

Cookies and storage. Your browser stores your cart and theme choice on your own device. Our admin area uses a sign-in cookie. Razorpay may set its own cookies while you pay.

Your choices. You can ask us to correct or delete your personal data, or to remove you from the newsletter, at any time using the contact details below. We keep order records for as long as the law requires.

Terms of use

By ordering from this site you agree to these terms. Prices are in Indian rupees. We may correct pricing or stock errors and will contact you if that affects an order you placed.

Our candles are hand-poured, so colour, finish and scent strength can vary slightly between batches and from the photos.

Candle safety. Never leave a burning candle unattended. Keep it away from children, pets and anything flammable, and burn it on a heat-safe surface.
